**Name:** Paris Ivaniven Ken
**Course:** CCM101 – Cloud Computing

Among the three cloud platforms I explored, Google Cloud impressed me the most. I found its focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes interesting. These technologies are useful for modern applications and give developers many opportunities to build scalable cloud-based systems.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because all three provide fundamental cloud computing services. They offer virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, security, and monitoring tools. They also provide global infrastructure that allows applications and services to be deployed in different locations. Although the names and features of their services are different, their main purpose is to provide flexible computing resources over the internet.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. A company should consider its budget, existing systems, security requirements, application workload, scalability needs, geographic location, and available technical skills. For example, an organization that depends heavily on Microsoft products may find Azure more convenient, while an AI research company may benefit from Google Cloud's AI and machine learning services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that selecting a cloud provider requires more than simply choosing the most popular platform. The provider must match the actual needs of the client. I learned that comparing services and understanding business requirements can lead to better technical decisions.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission because it now contains organized research about three major cloud providers, comparison tables, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, and screenshots. The activity also improved my ability to document technical information using Markdown and helped me understand how cloud computing concepts are applied to real business situations.
